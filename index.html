What Mossgate is

A new gate every day

The Daily Gate is one gate, one seed, the same for every player — and a different one tomorrow. Free, above the worlds on the title screen, with a streak for the days you finish it.

Thirty-six gates, rebuilt every run

Six worlds of six gates. Each world opens with its hand-built gates, and the rest are generated for that run — so most of the game is different every time you play it.

A different world every restart

Each run reshuffles the sky and the stars, where the coins and lanterns sit, and the timing of the moving platforms. Coins are also laid out fresh on every attempt at a gate. The gate is always reachable — only the way there changes.

Coins you have to jump for

Every coin hangs above your head, so it takes a jump to take it — and the layout is new on every attempt at a gate. No coin is ever placed anywhere a jump cannot reach, and the game checks that against its own physics before the level opens.

Built for iPhone

Native SpriteKit renderer, true 60fps on every supported device. Landscape only. Your thumb is the control scheme — or pair a Bluetooth controller.

How to play, in the game

A built-in guide on the title screen, illustrated with the game’s own art: every pickup, every hazard, and how your light works.

Plays offline

No accounts, no servers, no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

Privacy Policy

Effective from the date of first release. Last updated 11 October 2026.

Mossgate is made by JPHsystems, a one-person studio operated by Jean-Pierre Hermans in Belgium. This page explains what data the game handles. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

1. Data Mossgate does NOT collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone, address).
  • No analytics, telemetry, or tracking pixels.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA).
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or other device sensors.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Game progress: which gates you cleared and the best coin count per gate. Stored locally via Apple’s UserDefaults and standard Web storage (when running the in-app browser preview). Never transmitted anywhere.
  • Settings: your preferences for sound and SFX volume, plus the on/off toggles for each.

You can wipe all of this from the in-game Setup → Reset all progress action, or by deleting the app.

3. Game Center (optional)

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple handles your Game Center profile. Mossgate uses GameKit only to record achievements and leaderboard scores you earn in-game. We never see your Game Center friends list, your Apple ID email, or anything else from your Apple account. You can revoke the permission at any time in iOS Settings → Game Center.

4. In-app purchases

Mossgate ships with one world (Mossgate Dusk, six gates) free of charge. A single non-consumable in-app purchase — Full Game Unlock, product ID com.jphsystems.mossgate.fullgame, currently priced at $2.99 USD (Tier 3) — unlocks the other five worlds. The free world is a complete experience on its own; the purchase is genuinely additional content, never a way to finish the free tier. Each purchased world also stays shut until you have finished the world before it.

Payment is handled entirely by Apple’s StoreKit 2 framework. JPHsystems never sees your card details, your Apple ID, or your billing address. The transaction lives in your App Store purchase history, where you can request a refund via reportaproblem.apple.com.

If you change devices, restore from an iCloud or iTunes backup, or just want to re-install the app, tap Restore on the title screen and the full game unlocks again from your App Store purchase record.

5. Children’s privacy

Mossgate is suitable for all ages. It contains no user-generated content, no chat, no third-party advertising, and no links that take children outside the app.

6. Required-reason API declarations

Mossgate declares the following “required-reason” API access in its PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy bundle resource (Apple requirement since May 2024):

  • UserDefaults (CA92.1) — saving settings and progress.
  • File timestamp (C617.1) — reading asset timestamps during level loads.
  • Disk space (E174.1) — confirming there is room to write saves.
  • System boot time (35F9.1) — used by SpriteKit’s internal scheduling.

None of these accesses is used to identify you or send data anywhere.

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever change what Mossgate does with data, we will update this page and the version listed under “Last updated” above. Material changes will be reflected in the release notes for the relevant version on the App Store.

8. Contact

Questions, concerns, or data deletion requests: support@jphsystems.io. JPHsystems, Belgium.

Support & FAQ

Something not working? Most things are answered below.

The screen rotates to portrait when I launch the app. Is that a bug?

No. The splash screen runs in portrait because it’s a vertical poster (think of a book cover). The game itself locks to landscape as soon as you tap “Tap to begin”. iOS will rotate the device at that moment.

The game feels stuck on the splash. What now?

Make sure you actually tap somewhere on the splash — it doesn’t autorun. If you tapped and it still won’t advance, force-quit and relaunch; if it keeps happening, email support@jphsystems.io with your iPhone model and iOS version.

Can I play in portrait?

No. Mossgate is built for landscape only; the levels, the camera, and the on-screen controls are all designed around a wide canvas. The app explicitly disables portrait orientation for the gameplay phase.

Why does the level look different every time I open the app?

That is the point. Every run gets a fresh seed: the sky and stars are re-tinted, the coins and lanterns move, and the moving platforms run on different timing. The gate is always reachable — only the way there changes. To get another world without relaunching, open Setup and tap Shuffle.

Are the coins in the same place every time?

No — the layout is new every time you start a gate. Every coin is checked against the game’s own physics before it is placed, so wherever a coin appears, you can take it.

The coins moved while I was playing. Is that a bug?

No, that is deliberate. If you get twenty seconds into a gate without losing a life and with most of the coins already collected, the ones you have not taken move somewhere new — the game has noticed you are finding it too easy. There is a small warm burst when it happens so you can see the change was real. Lose a life and it leaves you alone for the rest of that attempt.

I left a gate half-finished. Did I lose my progress?

No. Going back to the menu keeps your coins, your lanterns, your checkpoint and where you were standing, and walking back into that gate puts you where you left off. It only resets if you finish the gate, reset your progress, or run out of light.

One thing worth knowing: relaunching the app gives you a new world, and a saved position belongs to the world it was played in. Rather than dropping you into terrain that no longer exists, the game discards it and starts that gate fresh.

Can I use a game controller?

Yes. Pair a Bluetooth or MFi controller with your iPhone, then turn on Game controller in Setup. Setup also shows which pad is connected and what each button does: A or d-pad up to jump, B or d-pad down for down, the left stick or d-pad to move, and Menu or Options to pause.

Where do I find the controls?

Tap How to play on the title screen. It walks through the controls, every pickup and hazard you will meet, and how your light works.

My progress disappeared after I updated the app.

Progress is stored in UserDefaults, which survives updates but not device restores or app deletions. If you restored an iCloud or iTunes backup onto a fresh device, the app will show a clean slate. There is no cloud save in version 1.

How do I reset everything?

Open Setup from the title screen, scroll to the bottom, and tap Reset all progress. This clears every cleared gate and best-coin count. Your sound, volume and controller settings are kept exactly as they are, and the app asks you to confirm first.

The audio doesn’t play. What gives?

Two common ones:

  1. The hardware mute switch on the side of the iPhone is on. iOS routes through that even in landscape.
  2. You turned sound or SFX off in Setup. Open Setup and re-enable them.

If the audio works elsewhere on the device but not in Mossgate, email us.

Can I get a refund?

Refund requests are handled by Apple. Use reportaproblem.apple.com — we can’t issue refunds ourselves and don’t see your payment details.

I found a bug / have a feature request.

Email support@jphsystems.io. Include your iPhone model, your iOS version (find it in Settings → General → About), and what you were doing when it happened. Screenshots help.

Is there a level editor / mod tools / PC port?

Not at the moment. Mossgate’s levels are hand-built and shipped with each release. If you want something specific, the email above is the right door.

Still stuck? Email support@jphsystems.io. Replies are best-effort but JPHsystems is a real human, not a bot.

Changelog

Public release notes. Current build: 0.7.32 (build 55).

0.7.30 12 October 2026
  • A daily gate. One gate, one seed per day, the same for every player in the world, free, at the top of the title screen. Coins are the score and the streak counts the days you finish it. It changes at midnight UTC rather than at your local midnight, so nobody gets tomorrow’s gate early — and it costs you nothing, because a launch has always been a fresh set of worlds.
  • Relight puts you back on the last waystone. It always should have. The function existed and nothing called it, so both buttons restarted the gate from the top and the guide’s promise — “you carry on from the last waystone you touched” — was never kept. The death panel now clears when you relight, instead of sitting over a game that is already running.
  • The difficulty curve rises again. The hazards in a generated gate summed past 100% from the third world on, so the step-up and step-down code below them could never be reached: worlds 3 to 6 were flat ground with gaps and brambles, and the game got easier the further in you went. Every world has its shape back.
  • Gates are roughly twice as long.
  • Coins stay off the brambles and off the goal. No coin hangs low over a spike, and none is planted on the flag — the flag is always on the grass.
  • Look up. There was a button to look down and none to look up, so you could not see what was above you before going back.
1.0 In development
  • Final pass on art, audio and balancing.
  • Game Center achievements and leaderboards go live once the Apple Developer team is wired up.
0.7.6 12 October 2026
  • A new app icon. The old one was a smooth vector coin, which never sat well next to a game made of chunky pixels. The new one is built from the game’s own art — the lit lantern from the checkpoint, over a dusk sky whose colours are sampled straight out of the running game.
0.7.5 12 October 2026
  • A coin you take now leaves the screen. The game drew each coin in one place and checked whether you had taken it in another, so a jump could count a coin as collected while leaving a different one sitting in front of you. Both are now the same place.
0.7.4 12 October 2026
  • Coins are worth going for. Every coin now hangs above your head, so walking past never takes one — you have to jump. The layout is new every time you attempt a gate, and if you are having no trouble at all, the coins you have not collected move to fresh spots part-way through the run.
  • No coin is ever out of reach. Each one is checked against the game’s own physics with an ordinary jump before it is placed, and there is a final fallback that cannot fail — so wherever a coin hangs, you can take it.
  • Fixed a crash where iOS could close the app while a level was loading.
0.7.3 12 October 2026
  • Leaving a gate keeps your place. Go back to the menu part-way through and your coins, lanterns, checkpoint and position are all kept — coming back puts you where you left off instead of starting the gate again. Finishing the gate, resetting your progress, or losing your last life clears it, so a gate starts fresh when it should.

  • Platform edges hold you properly. You used to be able to stand with nearly your whole body past the edge of a ledge without falling. Being held up now needs a real foothold rather than a toe on the corner.
